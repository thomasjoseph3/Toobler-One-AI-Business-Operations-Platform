**Status:** `approved` (18 Nov 2026, verify green: 2845 unit/API, 734 e2e; was `in_progress`) under the standing instruction (17 Nov 2026): built in slices of at most 2 routes, each tested and committed locally. Branch `feature/F11-views-filters-search`, cut from `dev`.

## Feature guide

**What it does.** As a business grows, the full list of leads or contacts is too long to scan. This feature gives every person four ways to find what they work on: **search** (the box at the top: type a name, a phone number or an email and see matching contacts, companies and leads), **filters** (narrow a list by the business's own fields, such as "Car model is Swift" or "Guest count is more than 100", or by a lead's temperature), **saved views** (keep a filtered, sorted Leads list under a name like "My hot leads" and open it again from a tab) and **lists** (a fixed group of contacts, like "Diwali offer 2026", for campaigns later).

**Why it is useful.** Each person sees just what they work on, without scanning hundreds of rows, and the same few clicks work on every list.

**A day in the life.** Rahul filters the leads: owner is me, temperature is hot. He presses Save view and names it "My hot leads"; it is now a tab he opens every morning. The manager shares a "Hot this week" view so the whole team sees the same tab. A customer rings; Rahul types "98470" in the top box and finds her contact, her company and her open lead at once. Before Diwali, Meera filters contacts by "Car model is Swift", presses Save as list and calls it "Swift owners 2026"; later she adds two more people by hand.

**Who can do what.** Everyone signed in can search, filter, save their own views and make or fill lists. Only a **manager** can share a view with the team or delete a list. A manager can also stop sharing or delete a shared view of anyone, but cannot change what it shows. Staff on "own leads only" find only their own leads, in search and in every filter.

**Settings and limits.** Search shows at most 5 of each kind and needs 2 letters. At most 10 filters at once. At most 30 saved views per person per list. A list holds at most 5000 contacts. A list is **fixed**: it is made once, and a contact who changes later stays in it until someone removes them.

**What it never does.** Filters never show data the person may not see. A bad filter is refused with a plain sentence, never ignored. Searching, filtering and saving views never change any lead or contact.

**Not part of it.** Choosing which columns a list shows (the Phase 1 plan mentions it; the designer's column chooser is not built yet), sorting Contacts, Companies and Tasks by any field (Leads can sort; the others keep their fixed order), and filters on Tasks beyond the due tab, owner, lead and person they already have (tasks have no custom fields). Lists used for campaigns come with Phase 4.

### For the tech team

Modules `core.search`, `core.views` and `core.lists` (all core; lists requires contacts, search requires contacts, companies and leads; none owns another's table). Tables `saved_views` (063), `contact_lists` and `contact_list_members` (064). Routes: `GET /search`; `GET/POST /views`, `PATCH/DELETE /views/{id}`; `GET/POST /lists`, `GET/POST /lists/{id}/members`, `DELETE /lists/{id}/members/{contactId}`, `DELETE /lists/{id}`; and a `filters` query on `GET /leads`, `/contacts` and `/companies`. The filter engine: `packages/shared/src/filter-conditions.ts` (the one check), `apps/api/src/kernel/filters/conditions-to-sql.ts` (the one SQL builder, parameters only) and `read-filter-request.ts` (the one reader that answers 400 `INVALID_FILTERS`). No jobs, no events. Known limits: no column chooser; no sort fields on contacts and companies; search is a plain `ilike` (measured under 200 ms with 50,000 contacts, so no index yet).

## Decisions (to be recorded in docs/REQUIREMENTS-REVIEW.md)

- **Search finds three things:** contacts (name, email, phone), companies (name, website, city) and leads (through the person they are about). At most 5 of each, so the box answers at once. The "own leads only" role finds only its own leads (as everywhere); contacts and companies are open to every role, as they are on their own pages.
- **Phone numbers match however they are typed** (`+91 98470`, spaces, a leading 0): the same rule the contacts list already uses (at least 3 digits).
- **The search module only asks the other modules through their `index.ts`;** it owns no table.
- **Speed:** measured before any index is added (the plan's test is 50,000 contacts under 1 second).

## Slice plan (at most 2 routes each)

1. `GET /search?q=`: the top-bar search box (it was drawn disabled) opens a results sheet grouped by Contacts, Companies and Leads.
2. `GET /views?object=`, `POST /views`: saved views of the Leads list (filters, sort, columns) with Save view and view tabs.
3. `PATCH /views/{id}`, `DELETE /views/{id}`: rename, share with the team (managers share), delete after "Are you sure?".
4. The filter engine: the same filters on contacts, companies, leads and tasks, including custom fields and temperature (no new routes; the lists take the filters).
5. `GET /lists`, `POST /lists`: static contact lists, made from a view or by hand.
6. `POST /lists/{id}/members`, `DELETE /lists/{id}/members/{contact_id}`.
7. `DELETE /lists/{id}`.

## Slice log

**Slice 1 (built):** `GET /search?q=` (any signed-in role; `q` is 2 to 100 characters, else 400). New module `core.search` (no table of its own; it asks the contacts, companies and leads modules through their `index.ts`). Finds people by name, email or phone (any way the number is typed, three digits or more), companies by name, website or city, and leads through the person they are about; at most 5 of each. The "own leads only" role finds only its own leads; people and companies are open to every role. No migration, no activity log (a search changes nothing). Web: the top-bar search box, which was drawn switched off, now works: results appear a moment after typing stops, grouped as Contacts, Companies and Leads, each a link; it says when to keep typing, when it is searching, when nothing matches and when the search failed; Escape or a click outside closes it. On a phone a search button in the top bar opens the box in a row under the bar. The top bar now sits above the sticky table headings (z-20), so its results are never covered. Tests: `search.test.ts` (8), isolation entry, `e2e/search.spec.ts` (phone and desktop, including accessibility).

**Slice 1, speed (measured):** with 50,000 contacts in the test database (`search-speed.test.ts`), a search answers in 177 ms (a rare name), 51 ms (a phone number typed with a space), 160 ms (an email start) and 55 ms (a common word), all under the 1 second the requirement asks for. So no search index is added: nothing shows it is needed (measure before optimising). Re-check if a business passes 50,000 contacts.

**Slice 2 (built):** `GET /views?object=lead` (any signed-in role; `object` is required and only `lead` exists so far, else 400) and `POST /views` (any signed-in role; body `{object, name, filters, sort, layout}`; the name is trimmed, 1 to 60 characters; the view is the person's own and not shared; the owner and the business come from the session, never the body; anything extra in the body is 400). A view keeps the Leads list's own filter words: `status` (open or closed), `stageId` and `owner: "me"` (so a shared view later means "my leads" to each person), plus the sort and the layout (table or pipeline board). A person never has two views with the same name, whatever the capitals (409 `VIEW_NAME_TAKEN`) and at most 30 per list (409 `TOO_MANY_VIEWS`). Migration `063_saved_views.sql` (`saved_views`, RLS, update limited to the editable columns, delete allowed). New module `core.views` (requires nothing). Activity log kind `views.created` (field names only, never the view's name). Web: a **Save view** button in the Leads toolbar opens a box to name it; saved views show as tabs above the toolbar ("All leads" first); a tab opens the list with its filters and sort and is marked when the list on screen matches it, and changing the filters turns it off. Tests: `views.test.ts` (9), isolation entries, `saved-views.test.ts` (6), `e2e/saved-views.spec.ts` (phone and desktop, including accessibility).

**Slice 3 (built):** `PATCH /views/{id}` (any signed-in role; body: any of `name`, `shared`, `filters`, `sort` (null clears it), `layout`; nothing sent is 400 `NOTHING_TO_CHANGE`; extra words are 400; a name already used by the owner is 409 `VIEW_NAME_TAKEN`) and `DELETE /views/{id}` (204). Who may do what: the owner changes and deletes their own views; **only a manager can set `shared`** (403 for anyone else); a manager can also stop sharing or delete a shared view of anyone, but not change what it shows (403, that is its owner's). A colleague's unshared view does not exist for the person (404); a shared view they may not change is 403. Sending what the view already has changes nothing and writes no log line. Deleting is for good (a view is only a saved set of words). Activity log kinds `views.updated` and `views.deleted` (field names only, never the view's name). No migration. Web: when the list shows a saved view, a row under the tabs names it with **Rename**, **Share with the team** or **Stop sharing** (managers) and **Delete view** (an "Are you sure?" that names the view); a shared view's tab carries the words "Shared with the team"; a colleague's shared view shows no buttons to someone who may not change it. Tests: `views.test.ts` (16 in all), isolation entries (PATCH and DELETE), `e2e/saved-views.spec.ts` (20 across phone and desktop).

**Journey tests (added after slice 3):** `f09-f11-journeys.test.ts` tells three whole stories route after route, with real people and their own sessions: the life of a custom field (add, fill in on a contact and lead, Won refused until the required field is filled, reorder, remove, hidden, purged after 30 days), a lead scored by what the customer said (hot with a reason, a person overrides it, the next conversation scores it again), and finding things and saving views (search by phone and name, the "own leads only" colleague, a view saved, shared, renamed, deleted).

**Slice 4a (built): the filter engine, on the Leads list first.** No new route: `GET /leads` takes `filters`, a JSON list of at most 10 conditions (every one must hold), in the address as `filters=[{"field":"temperature","operator":"is","value":"hot"}]`. A field is `temperature` or `custom.<key>` of a live lead field. Operators by kind: text, email and phone (contains, is, is not, is empty, is not empty); number (is, is not, is more than, is less than, empty ones); date (is, before, after, empty ones); dropdown (is, is not, empty ones); yes/no (is, empty ones); the temperature takes is and is not. The ONE check is `checkFilterConditions` in `packages/shared/src/filter-conditions.ts` (pure, tested for every kind): it refuses an unknown or removed field, an operator that does not fit the kind, and a value that does not fit (a number for a number, a real date, one of the dropdown's choices, yes or no) with 400 `INVALID_FILTERS` and the problem in words. The SQL is written once in `apps/api/src/kernel/filters/conditions-to-sql.ts` (every value is a parameter, `%` and `_` are plain text, a number answer only counts as a number when it is stored as one) and each list names where its custom answers live, so contacts, companies and tasks reuse it in the next slices. The "own leads only" role still sees only its own leads whatever the filter says. The board takes the same filters. Saved views keep them (`filters.conditions`). Web: the Filter panel has **More filters** (Add a filter: field, condition, a value box that fits the field, Remove) and **Apply filters** (only finished rows are sent); the conditions are in the address, count on the Filter button, are cleared by Clear filters, and are saved with a view. Tests: `filter-conditions.test.ts` (shared, 6), `leads-filters.test.ts` (10: every kind, every operator, combining, paging, the board, the staff role, injection text, every refusal), views with conditions, `filter-conditions-ui.test.ts`, `leads-view.test.ts`, `e2e/leads.spec.ts`.

**Slice 4b (built): the filter engine on Contacts and Companies.** No new route: `GET /contacts` and `GET /companies` take the same `filters` (a JSON list of at most 10 conditions on the business's own fields for that list, written `custom.<key>`; the shared reader `readFilterRequest` in `apps/api/src/kernel/filters/` checks them and answers 400 `INVALID_FILTERS` in words, and the Leads list now uses the same reader). They combine with the search and the lifecycle stage. The SQL is the same `conditionsToSql`. Web: the same **More filters** editor (moved to `features/shared/`) is in the Contacts Filter panel and in a new Filter button on Companies; a business with no fields of its own for a list sees no extra filter controls there. The conditions are in the address and count on the Filter button. **Decision (to record in R77): Tasks keep their own built-in filters** (due tab, owner, lead, person): tasks have no custom fields, so the engine has nothing more to add there, and the Phase 1 plan's "all four lists" is met by the four lists each filtering by everything they have. Tests: `contacts-companies-filters.test.ts` (4), `contacts-state` and `companies-state` unit tests, `e2e/list-filters.spec.ts`.

**Slice 5 (built): static contact lists, part 1.** `GET /lists` (any signed-in role: the business's lists, oldest first, each with how many live contacts it holds) and `POST /lists` (any signed-in role; body `{name, contactIds?, fromView?}`: the contacts to put in by hand (at most 500 ids), or a contacts view `{q, lifecycleStage, filters}` whose matching contacts go in once, or neither for an empty list; not both; a contact that is not a live contact of the business is 404 and nothing is saved; a view matching more than 5000 contacts is 409 `LIST_TOO_BIG`; a bad filter is 400 `INVALID_FILTERS`; a name already used, whatever the capitals, is 409 `LIST_NAME_TAKEN`). The list is **static**: a contact who changes later stays in it until someone takes them out. Migration `064_contact_lists.sql` (`contact_lists`, `contact_list_members`, RLS, members deleted with their list). New module `core.lists` (requires contacts; it reaches the people through the contacts module's `index.ts`: `findContactIdsMatchingView`, `findLiveContactIds`). Activity log `lists.created` (field names only). Web: **Save as list** and a **Lists** link in the Contacts toolbar; the box names the list and sends the search, stage and conditions on screen; a new page `/contacts/lists` shows each list with its count (loading, error with retry, empty). Tests: `lists.test.ts` (9, including the 5000 limit), isolation entries, `lists-state.test.ts`, `e2e/lists.spec.ts`.

**Slice 6 (built): static contact lists, part 2: a list's people.** `GET /lists/{id}/members` (any signed-in role; one page, 25 by default, of the list's live contacts, most recently added first, with the list and its count; **a read route API.md did not list: the list page needs it, recorded in R77**) and `POST /lists/{id}/members` (body `{contactIds}`, 1 to 500; a contact already in the list is left as it is and not counted; answers `{added, list}`; a contact that is not a live contact of the business is 404 and nothing is added; an unknown or another business's list is 404; a list passing 5000 contacts is 409 `LIST_FULL`, and the adding is undone; two requests at once take turns because the list row is locked). Activity log `lists.members_added` (no names). Web: the Lists page links each list to its own page `/contacts/lists/<id>` with its people (name linking to the contact, phone, email), paging, and **Add people**: a search box that finds contacts (the same search as Create lead) with an Add button each; it says when the person was added or is already in; loading, error with retry, "nobody yet" and "that list does not exist" states. Tests: `lists.test.ts` (13 in all, including the 5000 limit), isolation entries (read and add), `e2e/lists.spec.ts` (phone and desktop).

**Slice 7 (built): static contact lists, part 3.** `DELETE /lists/{id}/members/{contact_id}` (any signed-in role: takes one person out; the contact is not touched; a person who is not in the list, an unknown list and another business's list are 404) and `DELETE /lists/{id}` (**managers only**, 403 for staff: deletes the list for good, its people rows go with it, the contacts stay; the name can be used again). Activity log `lists.member_removed` and `lists.deleted` (no names). Web: **Remove** on each person of a list (an "Are you sure?" that names the person and the list) and **Delete list** for managers only (an "Are you sure?" that names the list; then back to the Lists page with "The list X was deleted."). Tests: `lists.test.ts` (17 in all), isolation entries (remove and delete), the journey "filtering, then keeping a group of contacts as a list" in `f09-f11-journeys.test.ts`, `e2e/lists.spec.ts` (18 across phone and desktop).
