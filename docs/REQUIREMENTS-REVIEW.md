# Requirements review

Issues found while reading `requirements/` (all of Phase 1, the top-level documents, Phase 2's README), with a
proposal for each. **Q** marks a decision the owner needs to make; the "Needed by" column says how soon.

| # | Issue | Proposal | Needed by |

| R1 | **Not every conversation is a lead.** Three separate, overlapping lists (scoring's "filtered", email's categories, ticket types), and `save_lead` lets the AI create leads by itself. | One per-business list of **conversation types**; the AI names the type, code decides the outcome (lead, ticket, log, filtered) and the alerts. `save_lead` becomes `save_details` + `set_conversation_type`. Unified `conversations` table for every channel. Full design: [CONVERSATION-TYPES.md](CONVERSATION-TYPES.md). **Approved by the owner, 9 Nov 2026.** | F14–F15 |

Fastify vs Express is still ours to confirm in F00. | F00 |
