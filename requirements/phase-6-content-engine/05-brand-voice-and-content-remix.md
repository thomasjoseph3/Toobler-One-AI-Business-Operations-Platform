# Brand voice and content remix

**Phase 6 · Content · HubSpot equivalent: Brand voice, Content remix, Content repurposing**

## What it is

The business sets its voice (friendly, formal, words to use and avoid). Everything the AI writes (posts, emails,
WhatsApp, replies) follows it. One piece of content can be remixed into others: a blog post becomes 3 Instagram posts,
an email and a WhatsApp message.

## Done when

- [ ] Brand voice applies to every AI-written text; remix produces all formats from one source.
