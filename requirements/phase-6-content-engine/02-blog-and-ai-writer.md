# Blog and AI writer

**Phase 6 · Content · HubSpot equivalent: Blog, AI blog writer, Content Agent, Blog research agent**

## What it is

A blog on the business's site. The AI suggests topics people search for, drafts posts in the business's voice, and the
owner edits and publishes or schedules them.

## Done when

- [ ] Posts draft, edit, schedule and publish with images, tags and an enquiry form at the end.
