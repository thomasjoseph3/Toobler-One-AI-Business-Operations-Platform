# Video and podcasts

**Phase 6 · Content · HubSpot equivalent: Video marketing, Create and publish podcasts**

## What it is

Upload videos (property tours, testimonials) and audio episodes; host them on the site with an enquiry button; see who
watched (for known contacts). The AI cuts short clips for social posts and writes captions.

## Done when

- [ ] Videos and episodes host and play on the site; views by known contacts appear on their timeline.
