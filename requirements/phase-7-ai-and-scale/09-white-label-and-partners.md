# White label and partners

**Phase 7 · Scale · HubSpot equivalent: Solutions partner program, Affiliate program, Remove branding**

## What it is

Agencies and resellers sign up businesses and manage them from one partner screen, earn commission, and can sell the
product under their own name and colours.

## Done when

- [ ] Partners see and manage only their client businesses; commissions are calculated in code; white-label domains work.
