# Prospecting agent

**Phase 7 · AI · HubSpot equivalent: Prospecting Agent, Lookalike prospecting**

## What it is

For businesses that sell to other businesses: an AI that finds likely customers (companies that match the best existing
customers), researches them, and drafts first messages for staff approval.

## Rules

- Messages go out only after a person approves, and only on channels and with consent rules allowed by law.

## Done when

- [ ] Suggested prospects show why they match; drafts need approval; replies become leads.
