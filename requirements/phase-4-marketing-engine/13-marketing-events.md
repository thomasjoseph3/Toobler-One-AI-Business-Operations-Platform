# Marketing events

**Phase 4 · Marketing · HubSpot equivalent: Marketing events, Webinars**

## What it is

Events the business runs (open house, wedding fair, webinar, demo day): registration form, WhatsApp reminders, check-in
by QR code, and follow-up of attendees and no-shows.

## How it works

1. Manager creates "Wedding showcase, 15 Dec" with a registration page.
2. Registrants get a WhatsApp ticket with a QR code and reminders.
3. On the day, staff scan QR codes. Next day, attendees and no-shows get different follow-ups.

## Done when

- [ ] Registration, reminders, check-in and follow-ups work end to end.
