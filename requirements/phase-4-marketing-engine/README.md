# Phase 4: Marketing engine

## Goal

Bring in more enquiries and win back past customers, mainly through WhatsApp, plus email, SMS, forms, ads and social.
A paid engine, switched on per business.

## Phase 4 is done when

- [ ] A business sends a WhatsApp campaign to a segment, only to contacts who agreed, and sees replies become leads.
- [ ] Forms and landing pages create contacts and leads.
- [ ] Reports show which campaign or ad each won lead came from.
