# Ads

**Phase 4 · Marketing · HubSpot equivalent: Ads (Google, Meta, LinkedIn), Ad audiences**

## What it is

Connect Google Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ad accounts. See spend, leads and revenue per ad. Send segments to
the ad platforms as audiences (e.g. "past customers" to exclude, "lookalikes" to target). Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a
chat the AI answers.

## How it works

1. Manager connects Meta Ads.
2. Leads from ads carry the ad's name. The report shows cost per lead and revenue per rupee spent.
3. Click-to-WhatsApp ads start chats with the AI.

## Done when

- [ ] Spend syncs daily; leads link to ads; audiences sync.
