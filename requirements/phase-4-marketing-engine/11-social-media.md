# Social media

**Phase 4 · Marketing · HubSpot equivalent: Social media management, Social inbox**

## What it is

Plan and schedule posts to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile from one calendar, with AI-drafted
captions. Comments and direct messages come into the inbox, and the AI answers enquiries.

## How it works

1. Manager uploads photos and asks the AI for 5 captions. Schedules them over two weeks.
2. Someone comments "Price for 2?" on Instagram. It appears in the inbox; the AI replies by DM and saves the lead.

## Done when

- [ ] Scheduled posts publish on time; comments and DMs reach the inbox; AI replies work on Instagram DMs.

## Better than HubSpot

- Instagram DMs and Google Business messages are answered by the AI, not only listed.
