# Phase 3: Service engine

## Goal

Look after customers after the sale. Complaints, questions and requests are answered quickly, mostly by the AI, and the
business sees how happy its customers are. A paid engine, switched on per business.

## Phase 3 is done when

- [ ] Tickets move through stages with owners and response targets.
- [ ] The AI answers common service questions from the knowledge base and closes simple tickets itself.
- [ ] Surveys go out after a ticket or sale and scores show in reports.
