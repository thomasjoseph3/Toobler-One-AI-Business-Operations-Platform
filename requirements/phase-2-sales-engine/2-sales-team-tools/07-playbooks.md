# Playbooks

**Phase 2 · Sales tools · HubSpot equivalent: Playbooks**

## What it is

Guides for staff during calls: questions to ask, answers to common objections, steps to follow, shown next to the lead.
The same guide teaches the AI how this business sells.

## How it works

1. Manager writes "First call" with questions: dates, people, budget, occasion, decision-maker.
2. Rahul opens it during a call and ticks answers; they save to the lead.
3. Reports show which playbooks are used on won leads.

## Done when

- [ ] Playbooks open on lead pages; answers save to fields.

## Better than HubSpot

- One playbook trains both staff and the AI.
