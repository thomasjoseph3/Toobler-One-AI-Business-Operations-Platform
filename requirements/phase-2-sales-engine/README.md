# Phase 2: Sales engine

## Goal

Turn leads into paid sales inside the product. The AI can quote, take bookings and send payment links, and staff get
HubSpot's sales tools: quotes, payments, invoices, meeting links, sequences, templates, forecasting.

This engine is a paid add-on. A business switches it on in **Settings → Engines** (the switch screen is built in this
phase; see [engines and plans](../phase-1-lead-engine/1-foundation/03-engines-and-plans.md)).

## What is in Phase 2

| Folder | Features |
| --- | --- |
| [1-selling](1-selling) | Products and price lists, quotes, quote rules and approvals, payment links and payments, bookings and cancellations, invoices and tax, subscriptions, contracts and e-signature, revenue reports |
| [2-sales-team-tools](2-sales-team-tools) | Meeting scheduler, sequences, email templates and scheduling, email tracking and mail extensions, staff calling, document sharing, playbooks, deal plans and buying groups, forecasting and goals, deal scoring and next steps |

## How the lead changes

The lead stays the one sales record. With the sales engine on, a lead can also hold: products and quantities, a value,
quotes, payments and bookings. Won leads with a booking or payment show it on the card.

## Order of work

1. Engine switch screen. 2. Products. 3. Quotes and payment links. 4. Bookings and cancellations. 5. Invoices
and tax. 6. Revenue reports. 7. Meeting scheduler, templates, sequences. 8. The rest of the sales tools.
Subscriptions, contracts and forecasting come last, when a customer needs them.

## Phase 2 is done when

- [ ] A business switches the sales engine on and the AI can quote and send a payment link on a call.
- [ ] A paid link marks the lead Won and sends a GST invoice.
- [ ] Hotels, travel agents and any business with dated offerings use the same booking flows.
- [ ] Every price the AI speaks comes from a pricing tool.
