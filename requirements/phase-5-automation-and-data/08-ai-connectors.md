# AI connectors

**Phase 5 · Data · HubSpot equivalent: Connector for Claude, ChatGPT connector, HubSpot MCP**

## What it is

A business connects Claude, ChatGPT or another AI assistant to its CRM and asks questions or makes changes from there,
with the same permissions as in the app.

## Done when

- [ ] Claude and ChatGPT can connect with a sign-in, see only that business's data, and every change is logged.

## For developers

- **Code folder:** `apps/mcp`
- **API:** [API.md](../API.md), Phase 5 → AI connectors
- **Code:** OAuth sign-in for connectors; the same tools as the staff assistant, plus the tools of each engine the
  business has.
