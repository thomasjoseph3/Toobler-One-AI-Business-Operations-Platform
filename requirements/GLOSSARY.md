# Glossary

Plain meanings of the words used in these documents.

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Account | One business that uses our product. Also called a tenant. |
| Activity | Anything that happened with a customer: a call, a message, an email, a note, a stage change. |
| Agent (AI agent) | Our AI that talks to customers on the phone, WhatsApp, chat or email. |
| API | The set of web addresses our apps and other software call to read or change data. Listed in API.md. |
| Agent tool | An action the AI is allowed to take, such as "save this lead" or "list the offerings". The AI can only do what its tools allow. |
| Association | A link between two records, such as a contact and their company. |
| Board (pipeline board) | A screen with one column per stage. Each lead is a card you drag from column to column. |
| Business questions | The fixed list of questions every business answers in setup, so the AI knows what it needs. Many answers are filled in from the website. |
| Business info | What the business told us about itself: address, hours, policies, FAQs. The AI answers from this. |
| Callback | A promise that staff will phone the customer back at a stated time. |
| Channel | A way a customer reaches the business: phone, WhatsApp, website chat, email. |
| Contact | One person the business deals with, found by phone number or email. |
| Company | A firm or family that contacts belong to. |
| Competitor report | A report for the owner comparing their business with up to three competitors. Never shown to customers. |
| CRM | Customer Relationship Management: the system that keeps every customer and every conversation in one place. |
| Custom field | A question or detail a business adds for itself, such as "wedding date". |
| Dashboard | A page of numbers and charts. |
| Endpoint | One address in the API, such as "list contacts". |
| E2E test (end-to-end) | An automatic test that clicks through the real app like a person would. |
| Engine | A paid module a business switches on, such as the sales engine. HubSpot calls these "hubs". |
| Hot / warm / cold | How likely a lead is to buy, worked out by fixed rules. |
| Inbox | One list of every conversation from every channel. |
| Knowledge base | Everything the AI knows about the business: business profile, offerings, situations, documents, FAQs. The AI answers only from it. |
| Lead | One enquiry from a contact that might turn into a sale. |
| Lifecycle stage | Where a contact stands overall: new lead, customer, and so on. |
| MCP | A standard way for AI assistants (like Claude) to use our tools from outside the app. |
| Monorepo | One code repository holding all our apps (API, staff app, public site, MCP server). |
| Migration | A numbered file that changes the database's structure. Each change gets its own. |
| Offering | Something the business sells: a room type, a tour, a service, a product. |
| Situation | A common moment the business tells the AI how to handle, such as a customer asking for a discount. |
| Owner (of a record) | The staff member responsible for that lead or contact. |
| Pipeline | The list of stages a lead goes through, such as New, Contacted, Quoted, Won, Lost. |
| Platform console | Toobler's own screens for looking after every business on the product. |
| Promise | What the AI tells the customer will happen next, such as "We'll call you at 9 AM". |
| Row Level Security (RLS) | A database rule that stops one business from ever seeing another's rows. |
| Sequence | A series of timed follow-up messages that stops when the customer replies. |
| Task | A to-do for a staff member with a due time, such as "Call Priya at 4 PM". |
| Template (WhatsApp) | A message Meta has approved in advance. Needed to message someone more than 24 hours after they last wrote. |
| Ticket | A request that isn't a sale: a complaint, a question about a past order. |
| Timeline | The list of every activity for one contact or lead, newest first. |
| Webhook | A web address another service (like WhatsApp) calls to tell us something happened. |
| Workflow | An automatic rule: "when this happens, do that". |
